For most of us, literacy is such a part of our everyday world that it's almost impossible to imagine life without it. But for nearly 300 million adults in South Asia, 68% of whom are women and girls, the darkness and shame of being unable to read, write, or do simple math is an everyday reality. The Women's Empowerment Project, started by Jerry and Marilyn Hogshead, is changing that reality for nearly 600 women in twenty adult literacy classes.
