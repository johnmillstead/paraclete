### Whitekirk:  New Life for an Ancient Church

Imagine a church that saw hundreds of thousands of pilgrims flock to it in the Middle Ages sitting empty and unused in the 21st Century.  Imagine a holy well sought after for its healing properties lost and buried in a field. Imagine over 700 years of history waiting for the next chapter to unfold.

Whitekirk church in East Lothian, Scotland is full of history, beauty and potential.  Now imagine this church renewed, restored, brought back to life for a new and glorious chapter of its long history.  Imagine a center of prayer, modern pilgrimage, worship and the arts.  Imagine a lost holy well found and re-dug; its refreshing waters flowing again.  Imagine with us…

![](https://www.paraclete.net/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/Whitekirk12.png)

Paraclete Mission Group has the opportunity to partner with East Mountain UK, the Whitekirk New Life Trust, Discovery Church Dunbar, Scotland and 24/7 Prayer Scotland to see Whitekirk purchased from the Church of Scotland, revitalized and transformed.  Money given to this project will be used for the purchase of the historic building, necessary repairs and restoration and the development of parking facilities and pilgrimage accommodation.

For more information, visit [www.furrsinkilts.com/whitekirk](http://www.furrsinkilts.com/whitekirk)
