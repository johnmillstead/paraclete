Tim Bedford is a mission-driven leader with over twenty years of experience in cross-cultural ministry. Originally from California and raised in Texas, Tim discovered his calling during a 2003 mission trip to Mexico, which sparked his passion for the Spanish language and serving the Mexican community. He holds a BA in International Studies from the University of North Texas and an MA in Cross-Cultural Ministry from Dallas Theological Seminary. Tim and his wife, Tonantzi, began their ministry journey in 2007 with SERVE International, a ministry of Denton Bible Church, in Monterrey, Mexico, and have since led initiatives in church planting, leadership development, and ministry growth throughout Latin America. In 2020, Tim joined Paraclete Mission Group and assumed the role of CEO in 2025, continuing his commitment to equipping leaders and advancing the Great Commission worldwide.
