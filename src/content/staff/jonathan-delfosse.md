  Jonathan Delfosse is the operations manager for Paraclete with a background in ministry, business, and leadership. He handles Paraclete’s daily operations, payroll, web posts, expense reports and various other tasks that keep Paraclete running. Jonathan has a vision to serve others. He hopes to assist the associates of Paraclete however he can. He serves at his local church in technical ministries and Bible study teacher. He’s based in St. Augustine, FL. 
  
   Jonathan’s ministry experience started in 2000 when he went to college to study to be a pastor. From there, he was a part of traveling drama and youth ministry teams that served the needs of churches in the southeastern part of the United States. Jonathan and his wife, Wendy, met in Bible college, got married and move to NC so Jonathan could finish his bachelor’s degree. In NC, he served at his local church as a youth leader and Sunday school teacher, and then as the media director for his church in south Florida. He’s currently a trustee at his current church in St. Augustine, Fla.
