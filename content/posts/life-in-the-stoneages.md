---
title: Life in the Stoneages
date: yyyy-04-Th
author: Fred Flintstone
---

Wwilma! I’m home! And boy, let me tell you, dragging that Dino-crane around the quarry all day really works up a prehistoric appetite. You’d think with all the modern luxuries we’ve got—like the new foot-powered 4-on-the-floor Woodsmobile and my trusty bird-powered record player—life would be a breeze. But between trying to avoid Mr. Slate’s temper and keeping Dino from tackling me the second I walk through the door, it’s a regular three-ring circus here in Bedrock!

Still, there’s nothing better than a quiet evening at the drive-in with a rack of brontosaurus ribs that’s heavy enough to tip the car over. Barney and I were just saying over a game of bowling that we live in the golden age of granite. Whether we’re heading down to the Water Buffalo Lodge or just relaxing on the stone sofa, life is pretty yabba-dabba-doo-zie! Just don’t tell Wilma I forgot to put the cat out again, or I’ll be spending the night on the front porch!
